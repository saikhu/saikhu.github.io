Computer vision and edge AI engineer focused on deploying deep learning models where hardware, latency, and reliability matter. I work across driver monitoring, occupant monitoring, road perception, surveillance, and medical imaging systems, with production work on TI TDA4VM, Hailo-15, Renesas R-Car, Rockchip, Mobilint Aries, and DeepX M1 targets.

At DELTAX, I led seven engineers across model optimization, deployment, and integration for automotive AI programs with Hyundai, Kia, and LG Display. My work covered embedded C/C++, OpenVX vision applications, Yocto-based SDK customization, Docker-based CI/CD for model deployment, and optimized inference pipelines that improved FPS by up to 10x over Python laptop baselines.

Recent engineering focus:

- Edge AI deployment on automotive and industrial NPU platforms.
- Driver and occupant monitoring using face detection, landmarks, gaze tracking, and drowsiness detection.
- MLOps workflows for model packaging, release consistency, and embedded deployment.
- Medical image segmentation and classification, including 3D CNN work on brain MRI.

My research background is in image segmentation and medical imaging. I completed an M.S. in Computer Science and Engineering at Chung-Ang University and published six papers across IEEE Access, Springer, and international conferences.

I am strongest in roles that combine computer vision, edge AI deployment, MLOps, embedded Linux, and customer-facing engineering. Current resume and detailed experience are available on the [CV page](/cv/), and selected delivery examples are on the [projects page](/projects/).
